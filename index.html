Why Dsectors

One firm.
Every capability.

Dsectors was founded on a simple observation: development partners in Bangladesh increasingly require research and advisory partners who combine methodological rigour with genuine field access to marginalised populations — meeting the compliance standards of institutional donors without sacrificing speed or contextual insight.

Our Board brings together, in a single firm, capabilities that are ordinarily distributed across several: a USAID Project Design Team Lead who facilitated ~240 projects worth close to USD 3 billion; a World Bank Social Development Specialist who delivered gender technical leadership across 50+ sovereign operations; a UN Women and Oxfam specialist with 27 years in gender-responsive DRR; a communications specialist with campaigns reaching 60 million people; an academic researcher in gender and discourse analysis; and a certified enterprise architect with 35+ ERP implementations.

📋
Donor-Compliance Fluency
Directors have sat on the donor side — designing PADs, serving as AOR/COR, assessing gender tags. We know exactly what World Bank, USAID, FCDO, ECHO and UN procurement requires.
🤝
Access to Hard-to-Reach Populations
Working relationships with community-led organisations serving PWID, FSWs, transgender/hijra and SOGIESC communities — populations conventional survey firms cannot reliably reach.
⚙️
In-House Digital & AI Delivery Capability
A certified enterprise architect on the Board — 35+ Odoo ERP implementations, NGO MIS, and secure closed-AI systems for zero-data-bleed environments. We build the digital systems that carry evidence into practice.

Our Methodology

A Proven Five-Phase
Approach

From inception to institutionalisation — we deliver end-to-end, not just the report.

01
Inception & Design
Clarify the evaluation or research question; agree theory of change; design sampling and instruments; secure ethical clearance and informed-consent protocols; produce inception report to donor standard.
02
Access & Data Collection
Deploy trained, gender-balanced enumeration teams; work through community-led organisations to reach key and hard-to-reach populations; apply safeguarding, PSEA and do-no-harm protocols throughout.
03
Analysis & Validation
Triangulate quantitative, qualitative and digital/netnographic data; test findings against alternative explanations; hold validation workshops with client, government counterparts and communities.
04
Reporting & Uptake
Deliver donor-standard reports, policy briefs and dissemination products; convert findings into BCC/IEC materials, campaigns and training modules; support the client in policy dialogue.
05
Institutionalisation
Build the client's own capacity — training, toolkits, MIS and digital systems — so the evidence base can be maintained after the assignment closes.
